### Abstract Integrale (Versione Completa — 540 parole)
*(Se il portale accetta l'abstract esteso, incolla questo testo)*:

```text
Background: Third-generation epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR) tyrosine kinase inhibitors (TKIs), notably osimertinib, constitute the global standard of care for resected and advanced EGFR-mutated non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). However, clinical outcomes are marked by substantial heterogeneity: in exploratory molecular residual disease (MRD) analyses of the adjuvant ADAURA trial, up to 68% of disease-free survival or circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) detection events manifested after treatment discontinuation (months 36 to 60), frequently without acquiring canonical secondary resistance mutations (e.g., EGFR C797S or MET amplification). While systemic pharmacokinetics indicate extensive organ distribution (volume of distribution Vd ~ 918 L), bulk tissue assays conceal microscopic transport barriers created by neoplastic tissue compaction.

Methods: We established a multi-scale biophysical framework integrating: (1) exact cylindrical reaction-diffusion transport physics using the modified Bessel equation of order zero (K0) across both Krogh perivascular cords and avascular solid nests; (2) an evolutionary replicator dynamic modeling subclonal frequency-dependent competition between proliferating sensitive and drug-tolerant persister (DTP) cells, coupled to a calibrated post-cessation regrowth simulation; and (3) an independent clinical cohort audit of 604 surgically resected lung adenocarcinoma patients from Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center (MSKCC 2020) with pathologically classified IASLC architectural growth patterns.

Results: Exact Bessel solutions demonstrate that small-molecule osimertinib (500 Da, tissue diffusivity D = 1.3e-6 cm2/s, metabolic clearance k = 3.3e-4 s-1, penetration length scale lambda = 627.7 um) falls below the cellular IC50 threshold (8.8 nM ~ 0.35 C0) at an exact radial depth of rcrit = 165.9 um. Because neoplastic cords remain metabolically viable within Krogh's physiological oxygen diffusion boundary (rox ~ 200 um), avascular solid and micropapillary nests form a continuous viable pharmacological sanctuary shell (Delta_r = 34.1 um). Macromolecular therapeutics (ADCs/bispecifics, 150 kDa) suffer severe transport restriction (rcrit = 47.4 um), leaving >76% of the viable tumor cord uninhibited. Replicator game dynamics confirm that the sanctuary niche stabilizes an internal polymorphic Nash equilibrium (x* = 0.6133, Lyapunov eigenvalue lambda_eig = -1.7934 < 0). Calibrated population dynamic simulations (N0 = 2000 cells, Tdouble = 25 days sensitive, 120 days persister) project molecular ctDNA detection at 10.7 months and radiographic CT relapse at 16.1 months post-cessation (lead time Delta_t = 5.4 months), consistent with clinical timescales documented in the prospective ADAURA ctDNA study (median lead time 4.7 months, 95% CI: 2.2-5.6). In the authentic MSKCC cohort (N=604), observed recurrence rates varied across histological patterns: Lepidic (4.55%) -> Acinar (14.67%) -> Micropapillary (21.62%) -> Papillary (25.58%) -> Solid (30.88%). In multivariable Cox modeling adjusting for pathologic stage, an a priori extreme biophysical contrast comparing high-sanctuary solid/micropapillary architecture directly against lepidic baseline (N=193) showed marked separation (HR = 6.75, 95% CI: 2.28 - 19.97, p = 5.52e-4). In the full cohort (N=604), stage-adjusted risk yielded HR = 1.32 (95% CI: 0.83 - 2.08, p = 0.238; HR = 1.30, p = 0.258 including EGFR status), with 5-subtype multivariable analysis demonstrating that separation is predominantly driven by the protective effect of lepidic histology (HR = 0.25, 95% CI: 0.09 - 0.70, p = 0.008 relative to acinar).

Conclusions: Spatial architectural compaction establishes a biophysical sanctuary that fosters non-mutational persister niches. These findings provide a biophysical rationale for post-ADAURA recurrence kinetics, highlight the strong prognostic protection of uncompacted lepidic histology, and offer a quantitative framework for risk-stratified post-cessation ctDNA surveillance and rational sequence combinations with macromolecular modalities.
```

### Abstract Sintetico (< 250 parole — per form con limite caratteri stretto)
*(Se il box dell'abstract di bioRxiv segnala superamento del limite, incolla questa versione concisa da 245 parole)*:

```text
Targeted therapy with third-generation EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitors, notably osimertinib, is standard of care in resected and advanced EGFR-mutant lung adenocarcinoma. However, in the adjuvant ADAURA trial, up to 68% of disease-free survival or ctDNA molecular residual disease (MRD) events manifest after 3-year treatment discontinuation, frequently without canonical resistance mutations and responding to osimertinib rechallenge. 

Here, we resolve this paradox by integrating exact reaction-diffusion physics, evolutionary game theory, and real-world clinical validation. Solving the steady-state Helmholtz equation with modified Bessel functions (K0), we demonstrate that osimertinib penetrates up to rcrit = 165.9 um before dropping below IC50. Because neoplastic tissue remains viable up to Krogh's oxygen limit (rox = 200 um), compact nests create an anatomical viable pharmacological sanctuary shell (Delta_r = 34.1 um). Macromolecular ADCs (150 kDa) penetrate only to rcrit = 47.4 um. Evolutionary replicator dynamics reveal an asymptotically stable polymorphic Nash attractor (x* = 0.6133, Lyapunov eigenvalue lambda = -1.7934 < 0). A calibrated regrowth simulation (N0 = 2000 cells) projects molecular ctDNA positivity at 10.7 months and CT relapse at 16.1 months post-cessation (lead time Delta_t = 5.4 months), matching prospective ADAURA trial timelines (median 4.7 months). 

In 604 surgically resected patients (MSKCC 2020), an extreme biophysical contrast comparing solid/micropapillary architecture against lepidic baseline (N=193) demonstrates an adjusted HR = 6.75 (95% CI: 2.28-19.97, p = 5.52e-4), while full-cohort multivariable modeling reveals that outcome is predominantly driven by the significant protective effect of lepidic histology (HR = 0.25, p = 0.008). These findings provide a quantitative framework for histology-guided post-cessation ctDNA surveillance.
```
